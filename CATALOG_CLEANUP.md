## Evidence and limitations

Forlagið product pages are the only bibliographic source. Every original entry has a six-part decision in [the machine-readable audit](Resources/catalog-audit.json). [Explicit per-ID decisions](Resources/catalog-decisions.json) distinguish publisher facts from reading-level judgments. Current product pages were fetched and parsed for every retained record, with source URL, date, HTML SHA-256 and a short synopsis excerpt. Same-title matches were checked against creators; series volumes were kept separate. The page snapshots are in the execution cache, not shipped in the website. Audit excerpts are concise, and do not substitute for reading the complete book.

Author credits distinguish explicitly identified translators/illustrators/adapters. Missing physical page counts and publication years stay null. Local covers were checked by their recorded SHA-256 and loaded in Chromium. Unchanged expansion covers retain their 2026-10-07 publisher verification provenance; newly fetched covers have 2026-10-08 evidence. The cache check reports current product pages separately from covers using prior evidence.

## Saved data

The static identity manifest reserves every historical ID. Saved and imported JSON/BOKASAFN data canonicalize duplicate IDs, deduplicate read/wishlist/challenge references, preserve both conflicting reviews and completion dates, and count each merged work once. Removed and quarantined books remain in history/statistics with archived metadata. Retained, duplicate-alias and archived `?book=ID` routes resolve; unknown IDs never resolve to a different book. A byte-for-byte browser rollback copy is saved before alias migration. If that copy cannot be saved, the original stored data is not overwritten.

Stored reading time, sessions, goals, achievements and challenge records are preserved. Derived page totals may change when publisher metadata corrects a page count or no verified physical page count exists. Conflicting reviews remain visible and portable; the canonical rating is used for the single active review.
